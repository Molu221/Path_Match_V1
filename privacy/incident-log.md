# Confidentiality incident log: how to use it

Law 25 requires every confidentiality incident to be recorded, and serious ones reported. **Keep the filled log private** (the team's
shared drive, not this public repository) and keep each entry **5 years**. This file is the template and the steps.

## What counts as an incident

Any access, use or sharing of personal information that was not allowed, or its loss. Examples for Path Match: a CV or email data
committed to the repository or shown in a screenshot; an access token in a log; a tester's data sent to the wrong person; a laptop
with test data lost.

## Steps, the same day

1. **Stop it.** Remove the file or commit (and its history), revoke the token, change the password.
2. **Write it in the log** (template below), even if it seems small.
3. **Judge the risk of serious injury** to the people involved: how sensitive the information is, how likely it is to be misused,
   and what could happen to them.
4. **If the risk is serious:** tell the **Commission d'accès à l'information** (https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/) and **each person
   affected**, quickly, and tell Prof. Ali.
5. **Fix the cause**, and note the fix in the log.

The person in charge (Loïg Glo) does steps 3 and 4. Anyone on the team can do steps 1 and 2.
