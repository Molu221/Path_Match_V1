The Role We Are Offering You

By joining our team, you will have the opportunity to support the Manufacturing/Industrial Engineering team. This internship offers hands-on experience to promote continuous improvement and contribute to strategic initiatives with a real impact across the organization.

Are You Ready To

- Lead and support continuous improvement projects by applying recognized methodologies such as PDCA, 8D, A3, Lean Six Sigma and BPM (Business Process Management).
- Participate in large-scale transformation projects that help define CAE's future.
- Contribute to Lean Manufacturing projects aimed at optimizing processes and ways of working.
- Develop process maps and identify inefficiencies.
- Conduct gap analyses to identify improvement opportunities.
- Identify, recommend and implement solutions.
- Create and communicate action plans to implement solutions.
- Organize and facilitate workshops focused on problem solving, root cause analysis, process mapping and other related topics.
- Monitor and report on project progress to help ensure the success of continuous improvement initiatives.
- Track and report impacts using key performance indicators (KPIs) and dashboards.

Our Ideal Candidate Has

- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Previous internship experience in continuous improvement, operational excellence, industrial engineering, project management or a related field.
- Excellent interpersonal skills, including the ability to influence, actively listen and collaborate.
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
- Asset: Six Sigma Green Belt/Black Belt certification, PMP.
- Bilingual (French and English).

Preferred Education

Currently pursuing a university degree or master's degree in industrial engineering or any other related field, with three years completed.

Location of the internship is Montreal.

Internship will be carried out in a hybrid format, with four days per week on-site.

Eligibility

All applicants must be legally entitled to work in Canada and must meet the requirements of the Canadian Controlled Goods Program (CGP).
