Your data on Path Match

Last updated November 17, 2026 · Version française

In short
  • Your CV is read in your browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone, and it is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • Your preferences, saved jobs and pasted postings stay in this browser on your device, until you delete them below.
  • We have no server, no account, no ads and no tracking.
  • Email features are coming.

What the site keeps, and where

WhatWhereHow long
Your CV textThis browser tab only (session storage)Until you close the tab, or press "Remove my CV"
Your choices: stage, places, kinds of work, languagesThis browser (local storage)Until you delete them
Saved postings, hidden postings, postings you added or pastedThis browser (local storage)Until you delete them
Profile extras: certifications, licences, languagesThis browser (local storage)Until you delete them
Job postingsOur public files on GitHubPublic job ads, refreshed every day

Nothing in this list leaves your device. Another person using the same browser could see it, so delete it on a shared computer.

Delete everything this site keeps in this browser

This removes your CV, choices, saved and pasted postings and profile extras from this browser. It cannot be undone.

What other companies can see

Like any website, the services that deliver the page see your IP address and browser type when you open it. They do not see your CV.

  • GitHub Pages (Microsoft) hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) delivers the code that reads PDF and Word files, and jsDelivr the code that reads a photo of a CV, only when you use it. That code runs in your browser.

When you open a posting, you go to the employer's or job board's own site, and its privacy rules apply there.

Email features

Email features are coming.

Your rights and who to ask

Path Match is a student project of Concordia University's Gina Cody School of Engineering (INDU 6993, Montréal), and follows Quebec's privacy law (Law 25). You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, get a copy, or withdraw your consent at any time.

The person in charge of personal information for the project is Loïg Glo. Contact: open a privacy question on GitHub. You can also complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
